01 / Motivation

Why Artificial Intelligence?

I have been fascinated by cars for as long as I can remember. Yet, frequent news of road accidents made me wonder: while the vehicles themselves could be made safer, how do we reduce the risk that comes from human error? When I first came across the concept of autonomous vehicles in 2015, I saw how intelligent systems could begin to address this problem. It drew me to AI, the technology that could give machines the perception and judgment to understand the world around them, not just react to it. Since then, I have wanted to apply AI to critical applications where reliability matters and the stakes are real.
